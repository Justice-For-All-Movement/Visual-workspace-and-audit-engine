# Private Knowledge record inspector

`projectKnowledgeRecord(context, owningSnapshot, selectedRef, stage)` (`./knowledge-review`) projects an explicitly selected entity, event, relationship or claim edition through the pinned owning Knowledge validators. It accepts only `{revision,state}` with the actual observed containing storage revision equal to retained record count. It validates full history, scope, record purpose and exact selected refs, preserving the owning private-history profile and separate null assessment/procedural/editorial fields.

The graph/table contain the selected record, its actual containing Knowledge storage edition, and one-hop exact captured subject/endpoint references and predecessor. Dependency inspectors contain their actual captured records and latest refs *within this supplied capture*. They are not expanded transitively. Historical entity editions are retained when a later entity revision exists; the view never substitutes the newer subject or treats a role assertion as verified. Latest/historical labels concern the capture only, not a live database. No Evidence, Identity, Consent or intake service was independently queried. Actor/time/request remain literal record metadata; there are no invented audit events, agents or permission decisions.

Input is capped at 3MiB, output at 512KiB, and the graph at 256 nodes/512 edges; violations reject the whole projection. Returned data is detached. Existing inert text/JSON graph/table inspectors are reused. The host must authorize and minimize the *entire* supplied snapshot, enforce current access/Consent/export policy and CPU/heap limits, and keep generated exports private. Snapshots and source hashes are not authentication; rendering cannot revoke downloaded bytes. Select the accepted owning Consent-guarded Knowledge APIs where source use is required. This pure capture formatter does not claim that their checks ran or turn historical metadata into a current permission or finding.
